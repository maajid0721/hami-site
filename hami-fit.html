HAMI FIT

A connected fitness ecosystem being developed for gyms, members and trainers. Our vision is to make gym operations easier while helping members build a continuous, portable fitness journey.

OUR DIRECTION

Fitness connected around people.

HAMI FIT is planned as a multi-sided platform with separate experiences and permissions for gym operators, members and, in later phases, trainers.

Planned product areas

  • Gym operations, memberships, attendance and payment records.
  • Member fitness identity, goals and progress history.
  • Trainer profiles, discovery and coaching services in later phases.
  • Role-based access and privacy-aware sharing between members and gyms.

These are product direction statements, not a representation that every feature is currently available. Release scope and access will be described as features become available.

PRODUCT-SPECIFIC INFORMATION

HAMI FIT policies and notices

The common HAMI Tech Labs policies apply together with the HAMI FIT-specific privacy schedule and any gym, member or trainer agreement. HAMI FIT may process gym records, membership and attendance data, payment records and fitness-related information depending on the features released. Access, sharing, retention, payment roles and responsibilities will be explained before those features are activated. HAMI FIT payment processing arrangements are separate from HAMI App and will be disclosed independently.
