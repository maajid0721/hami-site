WHAT IT DOES

Everyday family organisation.

HAMI App is designed for people who want a straightforward place to manage selected household information and routines.

Core features

  • Organise family documents and related information.
  • Manually record expenses and manage budgets.
  • Create reminders for important dates and commitments.
  • Use supported text or voice AI assistance.

Feature availability may depend on the app version and account eligibility. AI and extracted information should be reviewed before relying on it.
