Cookie & Tracking Notice

How the HAMI Tech Labs website may use cookies and similar technologies.

Last updated: 6 November 2026 · Status: Draft

1. Current website use

The current HAMI Tech Labs website is a static website. Its source currently includes externally hosted font resources and standard website assets. No analytics or advertising tracking script has been intentionally added in this website update. The hosting provider may process technical request logs for service delivery and security.

2. Cookies and similar technologies

Cookies or local storage may be used by the hosting platform, embedded services or future website features. Essential technologies support basic operation, security and user-requested functionality. If non-essential analytics, advertising or profiling technologies are introduced, this notice will be updated and consent controls will be provided where required by applicable law.

3. Third-party resources

The website currently loads web fonts from Google Fonts. A browser may connect to that provider to retrieve the fonts. Review the relevant provider privacy information for its processing practices. We will update this notice if other embedded or third-party tools are introduced.

4. Managing cookies

You can use your browser settings to view, restrict or delete cookies. Blocking essential technologies may affect some website functions. Browser controls do not necessarily stop technical server logs or all third-party connections.

5. Contact

Questions about this notice can be sent to akhan@hamiapp.in.

Maintenance note: Recheck this notice whenever analytics, advertising pixels, embedded media, consent tools or other tracking technologies are added to the website or a product.
