ABOUT HAMI TECH LABS

Technology with purpose.
Made to be useful.

HAMI Tech Labs is an independent technology venture based in Srinagar, Jammu & Kashmir, India. We are building digital products intended to make everyday experiences simpler and more useful.

Our work brings together practical thinking, modern software and user-focused design. HAMI App and HAMI FIT are two products being developed under the HAMI Tech Labs identity.
