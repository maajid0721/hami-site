PURCHASE INFORMATION

Billing, Cancellation & Refunds

This common notice explains how product-specific prices and payment terms will be shown. The terms displayed at the time of purchase or in a signed agreement govern the relevant transaction.

Last updated: 6 November 2026 · Status: Draft framework; product terms must be completed before accepting payments

Important: HAMI App and HAMI FIT are separate products. A payment provider, pricing model or refund rule used for one product does not automatically apply to the other. Do not rely on this framework as a final refund promise until product-specific checkout terms are published.

1. Prices and charges

Any applicable price, currency, billing period, taxes, renewal terms and included features will be displayed before purchase or stated in the relevant signed agreement. The price shown at the point of purchase or in the agreement will govern that purchase, subject to applicable law.

2. Payment processing

Payments may be processed by a third-party payment provider shown in the relevant checkout. The provider may have its own terms and privacy notice. HAMI Tech Labs will use transaction information needed to confirm, reconcile and support the purchase. Do not share card PINs, UPI PINs or one-time passwords with support staff.

3. HAMI App

HAMI App may offer paid features or subscriptions if and when they are enabled. The applicable amount, duration, renewal, cancellation route, refund eligibility and support process must be displayed in the HAMI App purchase flow before payment. The payment-provider onboarding currently being explored for HAMI App must not be interpreted as proof that a payment integration is live or that HAMI FIT uses the same provider.

4. HAMI FIT

HAMI FIT may support gym subscriptions, member payments or other commercial transactions in later releases. The responsible party for each charge, payment collection flow, platform fee (if any), cancellation, refund, dispute and payout will be defined in the relevant gym/member/trainer agreement and checkout before the transaction is enabled. No HAMI FIT fee or refund rule is created by this page alone.

5. Cancellation and refunds

Cancellation and refund eligibility depend on the product, purchase type, billing channel, service status and applicable law. The relevant checkout, app-store rules or signed agreement will state the applicable process and timelines. If you cannot find those terms, contact billing@hamiapp.in before purchasing. Nothing in this notice limits mandatory rights under applicable law.

6. Failed, duplicate or disputed payments

If money appears to have been debited but the product does not show a successful transaction, or if you believe a charge is duplicated or incorrect, contact billing support with the product name, transaction reference, date and amount. Never email full card details, UPI PINs or passwords. We may request limited information needed to locate the transaction.

7. Contact

Billing support: billing@hamiapp.in. Please identify whether your question concerns HAMI App or HAMI FIT.

Before enabling paid transactions: Publish product-specific price lists, tax treatment, renewal and cancellation mechanics, refund windows/eligibility, payment-provider details, dispute handling and responsible seller/merchant identity. Obtain legal/accounting review.
