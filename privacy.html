Privacy Policy

This is the common privacy framework for HAMI Tech Labs websites and products. Product-specific sections explain the additional information and processing associated with HAMI App and HAMI FIT.

1. Who this policy applies to

This policy applies to the HAMI Tech Labs website and to products that expressly link to it, including HAMI App and HAMI FIT. It describes common privacy principles. A product may show an additional notice, consent request or product-specific schedule where its features require more detail. If a product-specific notice explains a particular feature in more detail, read that notice together with this policy.

2. Who is responsible

“HAMI Tech Labs”, “HAMI”, “we”, “us” and “our” refer to Abdul Maajid Khan, proprietor of HAMITECH LABS, when acting as the operator of the website or relevant product. If a product is operated under a different commercial arrangement, that operator will be identified in the applicable product notice or agreement. 3. Information we may process

The information depends on the product, account type and features used. It may include:

  • Identity and contact details: name, email address, phone number, account identifiers and profile details.
  • Account and access data: sign-in/authentication records, role, permissions, device and session information.
  • Product content: information, files, photos, text, voice inputs or other content that a user chooses to submit.
  • Transaction and service records: orders, subscription status, payment status, invoices, receipts, refunds and support records, where relevant.
  • Technical and usage data: device/browser details, IP address, diagnostic logs, feature interactions, timestamps and security events.
  • Communications: enquiries, feedback, support messages and correspondence.

We aim to collect information that is reasonably needed for the relevant feature. Do not submit another person's personal information unless you have the authority or permission required to do so.

4. How we use information

Depending on the product, we may use information to create and manage accounts; provide requested features; maintain records; process transactions; deliver customer support; communicate service notices; prevent fraud, abuse and security incidents; troubleshoot and improve the service; comply with legal obligations; and enforce applicable terms.

5. Product-specific privacy schedules

5A. HAMI App

HAMI App is a family organisation product. Depending on features enabled, it may process account/profile details, family document files and metadata, expense and budget entries, reminders, receipt images, extracted text, and text or voice prompts submitted for AI assistance. Expense information in the current V1 experience is entered by the user or derived from content the user submits; the app does not connect to bank or UPI accounts as part of that described V1 workflow. Do not assume that every future feature will use the same data flows.

Documents or receipts may be processed to extract text or suggest expense/document details. AI-generated or extracted results can be wrong or incomplete and should be reviewed. Before launch, this schedule must be checked against the live app, AI providers/models, voice processing path, analytics SDKs, storage/retention settings and account deletion workflow.

5B. HAMI FIT

HAMI FIT is being developed as a fitness and gym management ecosystem for gym operators, staff, members and, in later phases, independent trainers. Depending on the modules released, it may process gym and staff account details, member identity/contact details, membership plans and status, attendance, payment and invoice records, trainer profiles, workout and fitness progress information, communications and support records.

Some fitness information, progress photos or health-adjacent details may be sensitive in context. We will request only information needed for the enabled feature, explain the purpose at collection and provide appropriate access and privacy controls. Gym operators may determine certain purposes and means for information they enter or manage. The respective roles and responsibilities of HAMI FIT and each gym must be stated in the applicable gym agreement and member-facing notice; this draft does not decide those legal roles.

HAMI FIT's final data categories, sharing controls, retention periods, AI processing, payment provider and gym/member rights workflow must be confirmed before public launch. Do not treat planned or roadmap features as already available.

6. AI, voice and automated processing

Where a product offers AI, speech-to-text, document extraction or automated recommendations, the information submitted for that feature may be processed by HAMI and relevant technology providers to return the requested result. Outputs may be inaccurate and should be checked. We will identify material providers and explain relevant processing in product-specific notices when those features are enabled. Do not submit information you are not authorised to share.

7. Payments and payment service providers

Where a product supports payments, payment details may be collected and processed by the payment provider presented in the checkout flow. HAMI may receive transaction references, status, amount, payer/customer details and reconciliation information needed to provide the service and maintain records. We do not intend to store full card credentials or UPI PINs on HAMI systems. The actual payment provider, data exchanged, refunds and settlement workflow must be verified and disclosed for each product before payments are enabled. A provider used for one product is not automatically used for another.

8. Sharing and service providers

We may share relevant information with infrastructure, hosting, authentication, storage, AI, analytics, communication, payment and support providers who help operate the relevant product; with a gym or organisation where the feature requires it and the user's relationship with that organisation permits it; with professional advisers; or with authorities and other parties where required or permitted by law. We do not sell personal information as a business model. Provider names and material data flows should be listed in product-specific notices where appropriate.

9. Storage, security and retention

We use technical and organisational measures intended to protect information, such as access controls, authentication, restricted permissions and security monitoring appropriate to the service. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot promise absolute security. Retention depends on the product, purpose, contractual requirements, legal obligations, dispute handling and backup cycles. Product-specific retention periods must be confirmed and published before launch; information should not be kept longer than reasonably necessary for the disclosed purpose, subject to lawful retention requirements.

10. Your choices and privacy requests

Subject to applicable law and the relevant product's operating role, you may request access to, correction of or deletion of personal information, withdraw consent where processing is based on consent, or raise a privacy concern. We may need to verify your identity and may need to coordinate with a gym or other organisation that controls relevant records. Some records may need to be retained for legal, security, accounting or dispute purposes. Product-specific notices will explain how to submit a request and any additional controls.

Contact grievance@hamiapp.in and state which product your request concerns. Ensure the grievance mailbox is monitored and that a response process is in place before publication.

11. Children and other people's information

Products are not intended for independent use by children unless a product explicitly says otherwise and provides an appropriate guardian/consent process. Users must not upload another person's information without appropriate authority. Any HAMI FIT feature involving minors must have a separately reviewed age, guardian consent and safeguarding design before release.

12. International processing

Service providers may process information in India or other countries depending on their infrastructure and service configuration. Relevant locations and safeguards will be described in product-specific notices where required by law.

13. Cookies and similar technologies

The HAMI Tech Labs website may use essential technologies required for basic operation and security. If analytics, advertising or other non-essential tracking technologies are introduced, the website notice and consent controls will be updated as required. See the Cookie & Tracking Notice.

14. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when products, providers, data practices or legal requirements change. We will revise the date above and provide additional notice where required. Material changes should be communicated through the relevant product or website.
